World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026: US-mediated talks between Russia and Ukraine ended in Abu Dhabi with agreement on a large prisoner exchange but no breakthrough; US envoy Jared Kushner called it the biggest prisoner release yet (Reuters). Iran attacked ship-navigation systems on Oman's Didamar island in the Strait of Hormuz, sources told Reuters, as oil rose on shipping attacks; 37 ships were attacked there in October, a monthly record since the war began. The US Justice Department charged three Iran-backed operatives over nearly 20 alleged plots in the US and Europe, including one at RAF Fairford, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent was moved to a military base over threats (BBC). At COP31 in Antalya, UN chief Antonio Guterres warned the planet is heading for more than 2.5C of warming and urged governments to tax oil companies' windfall profits (AFP). Millions of Spanish workers staged Spain's first general strike in 14 years over housing costs, before a snap election on 29 November (Reuters). Russia's ambassador to the US, Alexander Darchiev, died suddenly in Moscow aged 66, the Foreign Ministry said (Reuters). The US House voted 211-204 to refer impeachment of Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, killing it (BBC). Congo's Ebola outbreak passed 11,042 cases with at least 5,387 deaths, government data showed, while Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient and began a 42-day countdown to Ebola-free status (Reuters). Hot US October inflation kept rattling markets, two-year Treasury yields up 18 basis points as traders priced in a December Fed rate rise (Reuters). In Israel, Gadi Eisenkot, tasked with forming a government after the new parliament was sworn in, pledged to reverse Netanyahu's policies (AP). Hoax bomb threats emptied 6,300 schools in Italy, with similar emails in Britain and Ireland (Reuters). Salvadoran forces destroyed Haiti's 400 Mawozo gang headquarters, President Nayib Bukele said (BBC).
